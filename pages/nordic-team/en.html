Welcome to the SAS Nordic Team!

The SAS Nordic Team is a regional association of Swiss-Ski that is open to students who are passionate about nordic skiing competitions. In the summer, we offer training camps and coaching, and in the winter, our athletes compete in various competitions. Our main focus is on cross-country skiing, for which we provide professional support for the Swiss Cup racing series.

Additionally, we also promote athletes in other Nordic sports such as biathlon, ski orienteering, Nordic combined, and ski jumping, although we do not provide specific training and competition support for these disciplines.

What do we offer?

  • Monthly training camps from June to November
  • Race support including waxing and accommodation
  • Individual coaching for technique and training
  • Unique SAS Spirit

Who are we aimed at?

  • FIS athletes pursuing a dual career
  • Ex-FIS athletes starting an academic path who would like to stay attached with racing
  • Ambitious students (mass sport) when free spots are available

Our Competitions and Events

  • Swiss Cup series
  • Selected FESA Cup races, sometimes in collaboration with other teams
  • Winter World University Games (every 2 years)
  • Selected Swiss Loppet races
  • SAS Events such as the Pentathlon
  • Swiss University Sport Events
